# Maze Shooters submission kit

## Entry details

Title: **Maze Shooters — Missile Duel**

Description:

Two stationary players hide in a compact 3D maze and launch one steerable missile each, simultaneously. Create a private room, share its code, and play from a phone or desktop browser. Hold thrust, reverse and turn controls to pilot a missile with momentum and drift. Every corridor crossed stays lit on your own exploration map; your wall-impact splashes help you remember previous routes. Hit your opponent to win, or hit yourself and lose. Both players can request a rematch, which clears both maps. The game uses server-authoritative physics, supports reconnection, and requires no player accounts or installation.

AI tools and contribution:

I designed and iterated the game concept, controls, navigation memory and simultaneous multiplayer rules. I used OpenAI Codex to build and test the TypeScript/Three.js browser game and Node WebSocket server. This description must not be changed to claim ChatGPT Work was used unless that is true.

Project URL: **Pending a verified public deployment. Do not submit a localhost address.**
